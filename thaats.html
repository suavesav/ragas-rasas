The Ten Thaats

The parent scales of Hindustani classical music

The thaat system was codified by the musicologist Vishnu Narayan Bhatkhande in the early 20th century as a way of classifying the hundreds of Hindustani ragas into ten parent scales. Each thaat is defined by a specific combination of the seven swaras (Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni) in their shuddha (natural), komal (flat), or tivra (sharp) forms. A thaat is not itself a raga — it has no prescribed time, season, or emotional character — but a theoretical framework from which ragas are derived. A raga belongs to a thaat if its ascending notes are a subset of that thaat's scale.
